Asistente de WhatsApp

Tus clientes escriben, alguien responde.

Funciona sobre la plataforma oficial de WhatsApp Business de Meta, con el número de tu empresa y con la información que tu empresa define.

  1. Conectas tu númeroTu empresa autoriza a Doppel a conectar su cuenta de WhatsApp Business al CRM, sin dejar de usar la app en el teléfono.
  2. Responde con tu informaciónEl asistente contesta solo con lo que tu empresa definió: servicios, horarios y cómo agendar.
  3. Tu equipo toma el controlCuando la conversación necesita una persona, el asistente la deriva y tu equipo sigue desde el CRM.
  4. Todo queda en la ficha del clienteEl historial se guarda en el CRM, junto a sus datos, reuniones y entregas.

Agenda una demo
In English

Doppel CRM is a client relationship tool for small service businesses in Chile, provided by Doppel (Productora Domingo Ignacio Sandoval Piña E.I.R.L., Santiago, Chile). It keeps prospects and clients in one record (contacts, conversations, meetings, proposals, deliveries and history) and includes a WhatsApp assistant. E-invoicing with Chile's tax authority (SII) is coming soon. With the client's authorization, we connect the client's own WhatsApp Business number to the CRM through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform: the assistant answers frequently asked questions with information defined by the client, hands conversations to the client's team when needed, and keeps the conversation history in the client's record. Message data is used only to reply to the people who contact the client and to keep that history; it is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Clients can disconnect their number at any time and request a copy or deletion of their data at hola@doppel.cl.
